## 🕹️ About

Scavland is a hardcore top-down pixel-art survival RPG set in a post-apocalyptic Soviet world ravaged by war and the deadly Mist. Play as a scavenger exploring Zalesye, taking dangerous jobs, uncovering bunkers, building faction reputation, and fighting mutants and hostile survivors. The game is planned to enter Early Access on September 4, 2026.

Scavland is for players who enjoy demanding single-player survival RPGs, post-apocalyptic exploration, tactical shooting, loot management, and atmospheric pixel art.

## ✨ Features

- ☢️ **Wasteland Survival** — Manage scarce ammunition, radiation, equipment, and dangerous environmental hazards.
- 🗺️ **Open-World Exploration** — Explore abandoned settlements, forests, irradiated swamps, hidden locations, and forgotten bunkers.
- 🔫 **Tactical Combat** — Use firearms, melee attacks, stealth, takedowns, and weapon suppression to survive encounters.
- 🛠️ **Weapon Customization** — Build specialized loadouts with more than 25 weapons and hundreds of attachments.
- 🤝 **Faction Reputation** — Work with settlements and factions while your choices shape relationships and opportunities.
- 📜 **Jobs and Quests** — Complete contracts, main quests, and side quests to earn resources and advance your journey.
- 🏚️ **Procedural Bunkers** — Descend into underground locations with procedurally generated layouts and valuable loot.
- 🌗 **Living World** — Experience a day-and-night cycle where factions, creatures, and patrols can act independently.
